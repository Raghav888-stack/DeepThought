Proposal: How I Would Build a List of 1000 ICP-Qualified Companies in One Month



Instead of starting with exactly 1000 companies, I would start with a target raw universe of around 4000 companies. My assumption is that not every company collected from directories, Google, LinkedIn, expos or databases will genuinely match the ICP. Some will be traders, duplicates, too large, too small, inactive, outside the segment, or lacking enough public evidence.



So I would treat this as a funnel. Every day, I would process a fixed number of companies through stages: deduplication, basic pre-filter, evidence enrichment, AI-assisted scoring and manual QA. The goal would not be to force exactly the same number every day, but to maintain a daily processing rhythm so that by the end of the month I have around 1000 verified companies, with a buffer of 100–200 backup companies.



A realistic funnel would look like this:



4000 raw companies

→ 3000–3200 after duplicate removal and basic cleaning

→ 2000–2200 after rejecting obvious non-ICP companies

→ 1300–1500 after AI-assisted scoring and checking through trustable websites

→ 1000–1100 final verified companies after manual QA



This way, even if some companies fail in the final review, I still have enough backup companies to maintain quality without force-fitting weak companies into the final list.
